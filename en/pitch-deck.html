Hardware Strategy

Modular Reference Design

Eliminating per-market board redesign costs with the Universal Reference Kit.

We designed low-cost evaluation kits to immediately realize UniversalIotBus (UIB): Board A (right) focused purely on IoT connectivity, and Board B (left) consolidating core bus elements.

  • Board A (Host): Specialized connectivity board supporting ESP32-S3, SimCom, Ublox, and Nordic modules.
  • Board B (Bus): Standardizing Industrial-grade and Compact bus interfaces.
  • Geopolitical & US Tariff Risk Mitigation: Flexible component selection centered on US, Japan, and European (US/JP/EU) vendor chips (Nordic, STMicroelectronics, Renesas, Microchip), guaranteeing 100% NDAA compliance and zero US tariff penalties.
  • ASIC Silicon Roadmap: Free IP (RISC-V) + Gemini AI RTL auto-generation powering FPGA prototyping, culminating in penny-cost custom ASIC silicon fabrication.
