Key Development Track Record & Engineering Milestones

Representative system architecture projects across rail safety, semiconductor IP, police communications, automotive ECUs, and medical electronics.

Rail Infrastructure & Safety

100 Series Shinkansen Bullet Train ATC Control System (JR East)

World's first DSP-based rail speed signal detection software. Developed real-time signal decoding using NEC DSPs across 4-channel redundant dual circuits. Implemented 4-way majority voting logic (3/4 normal = continue; 2 failures = instant emergency failsafe stop), underpinning bullet train passenger safety.

Signal Processing & DSP/FFT

Subway Trackside Signal Analyzer (Tokyo Metro / Eidan)

Handled 30+ legacy and modern subway track signal frequencies. Replaced hardware LC resonance circuits with ADC sampling and DSP + FFT real-time frequency analysis, allowing instant dynamic frequency re-configuration purely via software.

Semiconductor Silicon & IP

IrDA Silicon LSI & Hardware IP (NEC & Agilent Technologies)

Designed double-buffer hardware IP covering 115.2 kbps to 16 Mbps wireless rates, eliminating loopback latency. Embedded in NEC's first notebook PC and mass-manufactured worldwide under the Agilent Technologies brand.

Police & Fire Emergency Comms

Full-Duplex Mic-Less Radio (Metropolitan Police / G8 Summit)

Developed earphone transducer system enabling simultaneous full-duplex transmission/reception without external microphones. Deployed by Tokyo Metropolitan Police Dept for VIP Protection at Toyako G8 Summit for silent whisper communications. Adopted by Tokyo & Seoul Fire Depts for zero-noise emergency dispatch.

Medical Devices & Beamforming

Aegis-Principle HIFU Fetal Therapy (National Center for Child Health)

Arrayed 9 High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound (HIFU) transducers utilizing Aegis radar phased-array beamforming principles to steer focused ultrasound energy. Enabled non-invasive fetal surgery without opening the pregnant mother's abdomen.

Medical Video & Real-Time Processing

8K Endoscopic Tele-Surgery Monitor System (Nihon Univ School of Medicine)

Real-time lossless zooming (8K to 1K resolution) for ultra-high-definition 8K medical endoscopy displays during remote surgical operations.

Automotive ECU & Simulation

Engine ECU Simulation Test System (HONDA Motor Co.)

Simulated accelerator, brake, fuel solenoids, and wipers in real-time to test HONDA engine ECU compliance during 10-mode fuel economy driving and extreme weather conditions.

Graphic Display & High-Power Control

Japan's Largest Yomiuri Giants Graphic Display System

High-brightness neon-tube graphic display board (16x16 dots, 1.5m, 8 chars x 4 tiers), rendering real-time graphic scores and animations for Yomiuri Giants baseball matches.
