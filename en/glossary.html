Simplified Technical Dictionary

Universal IoT Bus Glossary of Terms

Intuitive, Concise Explanations Tailored for Investors & Non-Technical Readers

This glossary breaks down complex hardware, software, and cybersecurity concepts referenced throughout the UIB portal into clear, intuitive definitions with technical supplements.

Universal IoT Bus (UIB) Core Standard
[Intuitive Definition]
An open, unified standard that allows diverse sensors, motors, and communication chips to be connected seamlessly like "LEGO bricks" for instant unified control.
[Technical Note] Standardizes physical & protocol layers to ensure complete interoperability across heterogeneous edge devices.
LL Grammar Zero-Parse Protocol
[Intuitive Definition]
A data structure that can be understood instantly just by reading bytes from left to right. Eliminates heavy parsing calculations while structurally preventing buffer overflow hacks.
[Technical Note] Fixed 16-Byte header based on LL(1) parsing. Enables O(1) state transitions and structurally eliminates Remote Code Execution (RCE).
Hardware Root of Trust (RoT) Security
[Intuitive Definition]
An unalterable silicon ID card embedded directly during chip manufacturing. Physically rejects counterfeit boards and tampered firmware from launching.
[Technical Note] Silicon-level cryptographic keys combined with Secure Boot sequence to guarantee software integrity at startup.
PUF (Physical Unclonable Function) Security
[Intuitive Definition]
A cryptographic key generated from microscopic, accidental physical variations in silicon—acting like a chip's unique "fingerprint" that can never be copied or cloned.
[Technical Note] Derives unique challenge-response pairs from semiconductor process variations, eliminating key storage memory leak risks.
5.0s Instant Cold Start Power Management
[Intuitive Definition]
The ability for a zero-power sleeping system to wake up, boot completely, and transmit critical emergency data within 5.0 seconds of detecting an event.
[Technical Note] Event-driven power-on sequence ensuring reliable data capture in off-grid disaster monitoring applications.
<100ms Physical Isolation Physical Defense
[Intuitive Definition]
An emergency circuit breaker that physically shuts off power to a port in under 100 milliseconds (0.1s) when an intrusion or voltage anomaly is detected.
[Technical Note] FET-switched power gating mechanism disconnecting physical bus lines to isolate malicious nodes instantly.
10μA Micro-Standby Current Ultra-Low Power
[Intuitive Definition]
Ultra-low energy consumption design that allows nodes to run continuously for years or decades on a single coin battery or small solar panel.
[Technical Note] Circuit optimization minimizing sleep leakage current for maintenance-free remote deployments.
Dual-Track Strategy Commercial Model
[Intuitive Definition]
A growth model balancing "public interest mission" (disaster prevention & open standards) with "aggressive commercial B2B execution" (ASIC sales & enterprise licensing).
[Technical Note] Neutral Foundation governance coupled with commercial enterprise adoption to drive worldwide de facto standardization.
