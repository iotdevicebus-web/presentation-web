Executive Summary

Universal IoT Bus (UIB) Enterprise Solution: Next-Generation Edge Infrastructure

Vision

Empower anyone to instantly build mission-critical IoT systems like LEGO blocks, even in harsh uncrewed environments lacking power and infrastructure.

Core Problem

Traditional IoT development wastes 60%–70% of total engineering time on custom per-sensor drivers and un-standardized hobbyist boards. Polling-based architectures drain batteries, rendering multi-year uncrewed operation impossible.

Innovative Solution & Proven Verification

  • LL Grammar (Zero-Parse) TLV Format: Unlike heavy XML or JSON, TLV processes data sequentially from left to right upon arrival using LL Grammar (top-down parsing), eliminating CPU parsing overhead and memory stack waste for true Plug & Play efficiency.
  • Zero-Trust 5-Layer Security & Physical Port Isolation: Comprehensive cyber defense incorporating Hardware Root of Trust (PUF), Secure Boot, LL Grammar buffer overflow elimination, autonomous <100ms hardware port power-gating, and NDAA-compliant trusted Western silicon.
  • Virtually Zero Standby Power (100% Event-Driven Architecture): All operations execute purely on hardware interrupts and event-driven power gating, achieving extreme 10μA standby current (virtually 0mW power draw). Guarantees multi-year battery operation in mission-critical uncrewed deployments with absolute peace of mind.
  • Empirically Proven Reliability: Measured interrupt latency <100ms (avg 88.5ms), cold start in 5.0s, and 100% completion in 19-burst stress image transmission via self-healing auto-recovery.
