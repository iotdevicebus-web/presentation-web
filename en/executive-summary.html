Executive Summary

Universal IoT Bus (UIB) Consortium: Next-Generation Edge Infrastructure

Vision

Empower anyone to instantly build mission-critical IoT systems like LEGO blocks, even in harsh uncrewed environments lacking power and infrastructure.

Core Problem

Traditional IoT development wastes 60%–70% of total engineering time on custom per-sensor drivers and un-standardized hobbyist boards. Polling-based architectures drain batteries, rendering multi-year uncrewed operation impossible.

Innovative Solution & Proven Verification

  • Pragmatic Plug & Play (Header Standardization + Payload Flexibility): 16-byte fixed TLV header handles instant hardware auto-recognition while allowing vendors full freedom to localize internal payloads. Eliminates rigid over-standardization friction.
  • Ultra-Low Power (100% Event-Driven): Dedicated interrupt line and power gating eliminate idle power draw, achieving extreme 10μA standby current.
  • Empirically Proven Reliability: Measured interrupt latency <100ms (avg 88.5ms), cold start in 16.5s, and 100% completion in 19-burst stress image transmission via self-healing auto-recovery.

Hardware, AI Workflow, ASIC Silicon & Ultra-Lean Governance Strategy

Universal Reference Kit: Modular evaluation hardware eliminating board redesign costs. Board A (Host connectivity: ESP32-S3, SimCom, Ublox, Nordic) paired with Board B (Bus interface: Industrial/Compact).

3-Step Custom ASIC Silicon Roadmap: Transitioning from evaluation boards (Phase 1) to FPGA prototyping via Free IP (RISC-V) + Gemini AI RTL circuit generation (Phase 2 / Month 18), culminating in penny-cost custom ASIC silicon fabrication (Phase 3: $0.15–$0.30 per chip).

Gemini AI-Automated Development & Specification Engine: Gemini AI handles both software development and hardware RTL (Verilog) circuit generation + testbenches, while automating vendor command integration to cut engineering overhead by 90%.

Failsafe Security: Autonomous recovery and power-gating isolation prevent local sensor faults from crashing whole systems.

Three Pillars of Execution: Architectural leadership by Gontaro Kitakado (Former President of IrDA USA, author of global standards & 21+ patents), Mission-Critical DNA (Shinkansen ATC safety engineering), and an Impartial Board with Alphabet participation to guarantee neutral governance.
