Alphabet Foundation Pitch Review: Anticipated Questions & Expert Responses

Technical Differentiation
Q1 How does UniversalIotBus (UIB) differ from existing standards (MQTT, CoAP, Matter)? Why is it necessary?

A: It solves a fundamentally different layer and target deployment environment.

MQTT and CoAP are high-level IP protocols, not physical-layer or power-gating standards for edge sensors. Matter targets indoor smart homes with frequent AC/battery charging. UniversalIotBus (UIB) is an open edge infrastructure unifying physical sensor interfaces, 16-byte fixed TLV zero-parsing, and 100% event-driven 10μA power gating for harsh, uncrewed, multi-year battery deployments.

Hardware register maps and protocol specs are fed directly as prompt ground truth, eliminating speculative AI inferences. Generated C/C++ code is immediately compiled and tested in CMake/PlatformIO pipelines to catch and auto-correct bugs or spec deviations instantly.
